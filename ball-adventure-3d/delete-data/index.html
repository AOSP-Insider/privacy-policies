Ball Adventure 3D — Data Deletion

Publisher: Samir Dubey (SDCode) · Contact: contact2sdcode@gmail.com · Effective date: 24/11/2026

Ball Adventure 3D has no account and no server holding your progress. Your save exists only on your phone.

Delete data on your device

Android Settings → Apps → Ball Adventure 3D → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the game. This permanently deletes your Career progress, Endless records, Coins, unlocked items, daily rewards and settings. A Remove Ads purchase stays with your Google Play account and can be restored.

Stop and delete gameplay statistics and crash reports

Turn off Settings → Usage data to stop new statistics and crash reports. To ask us to delete data already collected, email contact2sdcode@gmail.com with the subject "Ball Adventure 3D data deletion". Because there is no account, include roughly when you installed the game and your device model so we can find it. We act on requests within 30 days. Otherwise analytics data is deleted automatically after 14 months and crash data after 90 days.

Advertising data

Change ad choices in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices (where shown) and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Google handles ad data under its privacy policy.

What is kept

Google Play keeps purchase and order records under its own policies; these are needed to restore Remove Ads.
