Ball Adventure 3D — Privacy Policy

Publisher: Samir Dubey (SDCode) · Contact: contact2sdcode@gmail.com · App: com.sdcode.balladventure3d · Effective date: 24/11/2026

Ball Adventure 3D is a 3D rolling game with Career stages, Endless runs and daily rewards. You can play it offline, and it has no game account. This policy explains what data the game uses and what you can control.

The short version

  • No account. The game never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Career progress, Endless records, Coins, daily rewards and settings are saved on the device only.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase) help us balance the game and fix bugs. You can switch them off in Settings → Usage data.
  • Ads from Google AdMob are shown only to players who confirm they are 18 or older. A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off automatic ads.
  • Google Play handles purchases. We never see your payment details.

Gameplay statistics and crash reports (Firebase)

Ball Adventure 3D uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google. They are on for new installs and can be turned off at any time in Settings → Usage data. Nothing is sent while it is off.

  • Analytics records gameplay events: a run started or ended, the mode, world and stage, distance, time, Coins collected, deaths, stars and near misses, an optional revive, daily rewards and missions claimed, and items bought with Coins. It also receives a random Firebase app-instance identifier, device model, Android version, app version and the country derived from your IP address.
  • Crashlytics sends a report when the game crashes: the technical stack trace, device model, Android version, app version and a random installation identifier.
  • These events never include your name, email address or precise location. Ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation consent are set to denied for analytics, so analytics does not use your advertising ID or personalise ads.
  • Firebase keeps analytics data for up to 14 months and crash data for up to 90 days.

Advertising

For players who confirm they are 18 or older, the game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show ads between runs and an optional video that gives one revive in the current Endless run. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect your advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad views and taps, and diagnostic information. Google encrypts this data in transit. Where required, Google's consent message asks for your choices before ads are requested; change them in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Ads never grant Coins.

Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. Google handles checkout, payment and refunds. The game receives the signed purchase receipt, checks it on the device and stores it so the unlock works offline. It checks ownership with Google Play when it starts, when it resumes and when you choose Restore Purchases.

Children

Ball Adventure 3D is meant for players aged 13 and older. Ads and purchases are offered only to players who confirm they are 18 or older.
