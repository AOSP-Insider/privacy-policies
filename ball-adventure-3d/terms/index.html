Ball Adventure 3D is a free game. Career and Endless are playable offline with a save on your device. By playing, you agree to these terms.

Coins and game progress

You earn Coins in Career, Endless, achievements, daily rewards and daily missions, and can spend them on ball designs and optional powers in the game. Coins have no cash value and cannot be bought, transferred or exchanged for money. Your Coins and progress are stored in the game save on your device. Clearing the game's storage or uninstalling it removes them.

Daily rewards and missions

Daily rewards follow a seven-day calendar based on your device's date. Missing a day starts the calendar again at day one. Daily missions change each day. We may change rewards, missions and balance in updates.

Ads and the optional revive

Players who confirm they are 18 or older may see automatic ads between runs. When offered, you can choose to watch a full video for one revive in the current Endless run. Ads never grant Coins.

Remove Ads

Remove Ads is a one-time Google Play purchase that turns off automatic ads. Optional revive videos stay available. Google Play shows the local price before checkout. To restore the unlock on another device, sign in with the same Google Play account and choose Restore Purchases on the Remove Ads screen. Use Google Play Help for payments and refunds.

Fair play

Do not modify, reverse engineer or tamper with the game or its save to gain Coins or items. The game is provided "as is"; we work to keep it running well but cannot promise it will be free of errors.
