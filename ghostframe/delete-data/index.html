GhostFrame: Before & After — Data Deletion

Publisher: Samir Dubey · Contact: contact2sdcode@gmail.com · Effective date: 28/11/2026

GhostFrame has no account and no server. Your photos, jobs, clients and Brand Kit exist only on your phone, so only you can delete them — and we hold nothing to delete.

Delete data on your device

  1. Delete a job: open it, tap ⋮ → Delete job. Its photos and alignment are removed.
  2. Delete one angle: in a job, tap the angle's ⋮ → Delete angle.
  3. Delete a client: open the client in Clients, tap ⋮ → Delete client. Their jobs and photos are removed too.
  4. Remove your logo: Brand → Remove.
  5. Delete everything: Android Settings → Apps → GhostFrame → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

This deletion is permanent. GhostFrame's data is excluded from Android backups, so it cannot be restored.

Exported photos and Reels

Exports you save are stored in Pictures/GhostFrame and Movies/GhostFrame on your phone. They are ordinary files: delete them in your gallery or file manager. Uninstalling GhostFrame does not remove them.

Ads data

On the free version, Google AdMob collects device data as described in the Privacy Policy. You can change your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and manage data Google holds at myaccount.google.com.

What is kept

Google Play keeps purchase and order records under its own policies; these are needed to restore GhostFrame Pro. Questions: contact2sdcode@gmail.com.
