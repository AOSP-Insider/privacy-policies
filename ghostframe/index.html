GhostFrame: Before & After — Privacy Policy

Publisher: Samir Dubey · Contact: contact2sdcode@gmail.com · App: com.sdcode.ghostframe · Effective date: 28/11/2026

GhostFrame is a camera for service professionals that shows your Before photo as a see-through ghost so your After matches it, then turns the pair into a branded collage or Reel. It was built so that your clients' photos never leave your phone. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data.

The short version

  • No account. GhostFrame never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your photos stay on your phone. Before and After photos, clients, jobs and your logo are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • No analytics and no crash-reporting SDK.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data (below). GhostFrame Pro has no ads and does not start the ads SDK.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What stays on your phone

GhostFrame stores the following only in its private on-device storage. None of it is sent to us or anyone else:

DataWhy
Before and After photosThe photos you take in the app or import, kept at full quality so exports look sharp.
Jobs and anglesJob names, service tags, angle labels, shot details (zoom, lens, frame shape, tilt) and alignment, so the After camera can match the Before.
ClientsClient names and notes you add, to group repeat visits.
Brand KitYour logo, social handle and label preferences, added to your exports.
SettingsApp preferences and your Pro status, cached so Pro keeps working offline.

Android backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for GhostFrame, so none of this is copied to the cloud. Exports you choose to save go to your gallery: Pictures/GhostFrame and Movies/GhostFrame.

Uninstalling GhostFrame, or clearing its storage, deletes everything in its private storage permanently. We cannot recover it, because we never had it.

How auto-align works

Auto-align lines up your After with your Before by matching details in the two photos, and by finding the face in portraits. It uses the OpenCV library and a small face-detection model bundled inside the app, and runs entirely on your phone. Photos are analysed in memory and nothing is sent anywhere.

GhostFrame does not ask for access to all your photos — imports use Android's photo picker, which shares only the photo you choose.

Ads on the free version

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the Clients screen and, now and then, an ad after you save an export. Ads never appear in the camera. To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location;
  • interactions with the app and with ads, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance;
  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and it is encrypted in transit. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and wherever the law requires it, GhostFrame shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

With GhostFrame Pro the ads SDK is not started, so none of the above is collected.

Purchases

GhostFrame Pro is sold through Google Play Billing as a monthly or yearly subscription. Google processes payments, renewals, refunds and restoration. GhostFrame receives only the subscription status, which it caches on the phone so Pro keeps working offline.

Crash information

GhostFrame includes no crash-reporting or analytics SDK. If you allow it in your device settings, Google Play may share anonymous crash and performance data with developers (Android vitals); this is controlled by Google and your device.

Sharing

Photos and videos leave the app only when you share an export, and only to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or Instagram). That app's own privacy policy then applies.

Third parties

Google Play, Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (the consent form) are provided by Google under its own terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. OpenCV and the face-detection model run offline inside GhostFrame.

Children

GhostFrame is a tool for businesses and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — the app sends none to us.

Security and your choices

Your photos and jobs live in GhostFrame's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can delete any job, angle or client in the app, or delete everything at once — see Data deletion.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above shows the current version; material changes are also noted in the app's release notes.
