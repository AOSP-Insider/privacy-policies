SmoothRec: Screen Recorder Pro — Privacy Policy

Publisher: Samir Dubey · Contact: contact2sdcode@gmail.com · App: com.sdcode.smoothrec · Effective date: 25/11/2026

SmoothRec turns screen recordings into polished demos — a phone frame on a designed background, camera zooms on your taps, and private info hidden automatically. It was built so that your recordings never leave your phone. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data.

The short version

  • No account. SmoothRec never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • No internet. The app does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send your videos, recordings, audio, detected text or settings anywhere.
  • No ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting SDK.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What stays on your phone

SmoothRec stores the following only in its private on-device storage. None of it is sent to us or anyone else:

DataWhy
Screen recordings and imported videosThe source of each project. Imported videos are copied in, so editing never changes your original.
ProjectsTitles, style, zooms, audio settings and your edit history, so you can come back to a video.
Hidden itemsWhere private info appears on screen (box positions and times) and a masked preview such as "s•••@gmail.com" so you can review it. The detected text itself is never stored.
"My words"Words you ask SmoothRec to hide in every video (your name, address, company). Encrypted with AES-256 using a key kept in Android Keystore.
VoiceoversNarration you record in the Audio tab.
SettingsDefaults such as audio mode, frame rate and export quality.

Android backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for SmoothRec, so none of this is copied to the cloud. Exported videos are saved where you can see and manage them: Movies/SmoothRec in your gallery.

Uninstalling SmoothRec, or clearing its storage, deletes everything in its private storage permanently. We cannot recover it, because we never had it.

How private info is found

SmoothRec looks for emails, phone numbers, one-time codes, UPI IDs, amounts, card numbers, API keys and your own words using Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs entirely on your phone with a model bundled inside the app. Video frames are analysed in memory and discarded. Automatic detection is a helper, not a guarantee — always review your video before you share it; you can add blur boxes by hand.

Permissions and what they are used for

PermissionUsed for
Screen capture (MediaProjection)Recording your screen. Android asks for your consent every time you start a recording and shows that recording is on.
MicrophoneOnly if you choose to record your voice, app sound or a voiceover.
NotificationsRecording controls (Pause, Stop), "Recording saved" and export progress.
Display over other apps (optional)The small floating stop button while you record.
Foreground servicesTo keep recording and exporting while you use other apps.
Google Play billingTo buy and restore SmoothRec Pro.

SmoothRec does not use Accessibility services and does not ask for access to all your photos — imports use Android's photo picker, which shares only the video you choose.

Purchases

SmoothRec Pro is sold through Google Play Billing (a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase). Google processes payments, renewals, refunds and restoration. SmoothRec receives only the purchase status, which it caches on the phone so Pro keeps working offline.

Crash information

SmoothRec includes no crash-reporting or analytics SDK. If you allow it in your device settings, Google Play may share anonymous crash and performance data with developers (Android vitals); this is controlled by Google and your device.

Third parties

Google Play and the on-device ML Kit library are provided by Google under its own terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. ML Kit's bundled text recognition runs offline in SmoothRec.

Children

SmoothRec is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — the app collects none.

Security and your choices

Your projects live in SmoothRec's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can delete any project, voiceover or word in the app, or delete everything at once — see Data deletion.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above shows the current version; material changes are also noted in the app's release notes.
