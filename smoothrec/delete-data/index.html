SmoothRec: Screen Recorder Pro — Data Deletion

Publisher: Samir Dubey · Contact: contact2sdcode@gmail.com · Effective date: 25/11/2026

SmoothRec has no account, no internet permission and no server. Your recordings, projects, voiceovers and "My words" exist only on your phone, so only you can delete them — and we hold nothing to delete.

Delete data on your device

  1. Delete a project: long-press it in the Library → Delete. Its recording, thumbnail, zooms, hidden items and voiceover are removed (files another project still uses are kept).
  2. Delete words: Settings → My words, then remove any word.
  3. Delete everything: Android Settings → Apps → SmoothRec → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

This deletion is permanent. SmoothRec's data is excluded from Android backups, so it cannot be restored.

Exported videos

Videos you export are saved to Movies/SmoothRec on your phone. They are ordinary files: delete them in your gallery or file manager. Uninstalling SmoothRec does not remove them.

What is kept

Google Play keeps purchase and order records under its own policies; these are needed to restore SmoothRec Pro. Questions: contact2sdcode@gmail.com.
