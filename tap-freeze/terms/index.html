What TAP Freeze does — and doesn't

TAP Freeze helps you keep chosen apps closed until you tap a card or QR Key you linked. It is a focus aid, not a security, parental-control or safety product: it cannot stop someone who is determined to get around it (for example by restarting in safe mode or uninstalling apps without Hard Freeze), and it depends on Android permissions you can switch off. Phone calls, alarms and your home screen always stay available. Emergency Thaw is always offered so you are never locked out; it is limited to 3 uses every 30 days, plus a slower typed-promise option.

Keys

Use a card you are entitled to carry (Metro card, office badge, hotel key, NFC sticker) or a printed QR Key. TAP Freeze only reads the card's ID; it never writes to, charges or changes your card. Bank and credit cards cannot be used. Keep your key somewhere you can reach when you need it; if it is lost, use Emergency Thaw and link a new key.
