TAP Freeze: NFC App Blocker — Data Deletion

Publisher: Samir Dubey · Contact: contact2sdcode@gmail.com · Effective date: 24/11/2026

TAP Freeze has no account and no server holding your keys, modes or focus history — that data exists only on your phone.

Delete data on your device

  1. Remove individual keys in You → Keys and modes in Modes, or
  2. Android Settings → Apps → TAP Freeze → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

This permanently deletes keys, modes, schedules, sessions and Insights. Thaw first if a freeze is running.

Stop and delete crash reports and usage stats

Turn off You → Share crash reports and usage stats to stop new reports. To ask us to delete data already collected, email contact2sdcode@gmail.com with the subject "TAP Freeze data deletion". Because there is no account, include the date you installed the app and your device model so we can find it. We act on requests within 30 days. Otherwise analytics data is deleted automatically after 14 months and crash data after 90 days.

Advertising data

Change ad choices in You → Ad privacy choices (where shown) and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Google handles ad data under its privacy policy.

What is kept

Google Play keeps purchase and order records under its own policies; these are needed to restore TAP Freeze Pro.
