TAP Freeze: NFC App Blocker — Privacy Policy

Publisher: Samir Dubey · Contact: contact2sdcode@gmail.com · App: com.sdcode.tapfreeze · Effective date: 24/11/2026 · Last updated: 28/11/2026

TAP Freeze lets you freeze distracting apps with a card you already own (or a printed QR Key), so only that card brings them back. It was built so that your focus data stays on your phone. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data.

The short version

  • No account. TAP Freeze never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your keys, modes, app list and focus history never leave your phone. We run no server that receives them, and they are excluded from Android backups.
  • Anonymous crash reports and usage stats (Google Firebase) help fix bugs. They never include your apps, keys, modes or focus history, and you can switch them off.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. TAP Freeze Pro has no ads and never starts the ad SDK.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What stays on your phone

TAP Freeze stores the following only in its private on-device database. None of it is sent to us or anyone else:

DataWhy
Key fingerprintsA one-way keyed hash of your NFC card's ID, or the token in your QR Key. The card's actual ID is never stored. The hashing key is generated on your phone and protected by Android Keystore.
Modes and the apps in themSo TAP Freeze knows what to freeze.
Focus sessions, "reaches" and Emergency Thaw historyTo show Insights and enforce the Emergency Thaw limit.

Uninstalling TAP Freeze, or clearing its storage, deletes all of it permanently. We cannot recover it, because we never had it.

Crash reports and usage stats (Firebase)

TAP Freeze uses Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google. They are on for new installs and can be turned off any time in You → Share crash reports and usage stats; nothing is sent while it is off.

  • Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes: the technical stack trace, device model, Android version, app version and a random installation identifier.
  • Analytics records a few product events — for example that a freeze started or ended, that a key was linked, or that the Pro screen was opened — with only general values such as the mode template ("Sleep", "Deep Work"), card or QR, and rough ranges ("1–3 apps", "30 min – 2 h"). It never includes the names of your apps, keys or modes, or your focus history. Analytics is configured not to collect the advertising ID, not to personalise ads and not to track screens automatically.
  • Firebase keeps analytics data for up to 14 months and crash data for up to 90 days.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): one banner on the Insights screen and, at most once a day, a full-screen ad after you thaw. To show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud, Google may process your advertising ID, IP-derived general location, ad views and taps, and diagnostic data, including crash logs. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and applicable US states, Google's consent message (UMP) asks for your choices before ads are requested; change them in You → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. TAP Freeze Pro removes ads completely.

TAP Freeze never passes your keys, modes, app list, usage history or focus data to the ad SDK or to Firebase.

Purchases

TAP Freeze Pro is sold through Google Play Billing (monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase). Google processes payments, renewals, refunds and restoration. TAP Freeze receives only a purchase token and the purchase status, which it caches on the phone so Pro keeps working offline.

Third parties

Google processes data under its own terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Firebase privacy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Children

TAP Freeze is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child's data was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and your choices

On-device data lives in TAP Freeze's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Key fingerprints are keyed hashes and cannot be turned back into your card's ID. Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. You can switch off crash reports and usage stats, change ad privacy choices, delete keys and modes, or ask us to delete data — see Data deletion.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date above shows the current version; material changes are also noted in the app's release notes.
