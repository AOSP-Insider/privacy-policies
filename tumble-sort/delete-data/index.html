Tumble Sort: Gravity Box

Delete your data · Developer: Samir Dubey · com.sdcode.tumblesort

Data on your phone (progress, settings, boosters)

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Tumble Sort.
  2. Tap Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the game.

This deletes all game progress immediately. Purchases stay linked to your Google account and are restored on reinstall.

Analytics data (Google Analytics for Firebase)

  1. Open an issue at github.com/AOSP-Insider/privacy-policies with the title "Tumble Sort data deletion" and the approximate date you installed the game.
  2. We delete the matching analytics data within 30 days and confirm in the issue.

Analytics data is anonymous and is deleted automatically after 14 months even without a request.

Advertising data

Ads are served by Google AdMob. Manage or delete ad data at My Ad Center, and reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
