JoyDeck: Offline Mini Games — Data deletion

Developer: Samir Dubey (Shivoham Play) · Contact: contact2sdcode@gmail.com

JoyDeck has no accounts. Most data lives only on your device.

Delete data on your device

  1. Open JoyDeck → Settings → Privacy → Reset local progress, or
  2. Android Settings → Apps → JoyDeck → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Stop and delete analytics data

Turn off Settings → Privacy → Analytics to stop new analytics and crash reports. To ask us to delete analytics data already collected, email contact2sdcode@gmail.com with the subject "JoyDeck data deletion". Because there is no account, include the date you installed the app and your device model so we can find it. We act on requests within 30 days. Analytics data is otherwise deleted automatically after 14 months and crash data after 90 days.

What is kept

Google Play keeps purchase and order records under its own policies; these are needed to restore one-time purchases.
