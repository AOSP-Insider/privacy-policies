JoyDeck: Offline Mini Games — Privacy Policy

Publisher: Samir Dubey · Contact: contact2sdcode@gmail.com · Effective date: 21/11/2026

The game and local data

JoyDeck is a free collection of offline mini games. You do not need an account and there is no sign-in. Game progress, scores, Stars, Coins, favourites, themes and settings are stored on your device. Signed Google Play purchase records are kept so one-time purchases work offline. We do not run our own game server or account database.

Use Settings → Privacy → Reset local progress in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove this local data. This does not erase purchase records held by Google Play; purchases can be restored with the same Google Play account.

Analytics and crash reports

To understand which games people enjoy and to fix problems, JoyDeck uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. They are on for new installs and can be turned off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Analytics. When on, they may collect:

  • app activity such as games started and finished, levels completed, scores, boosters used, ads shown and in-app purchase results;
  • crash and error reports, including device model, Android version, app version and the state of the app at the time of the crash;
  • an app-instance identifier and, where you allow it, the Android advertising ID; general (IP-derived, not precise) location.

This data is not used to identify you personally and is not sold. Firebase retains analytics data for up to 14 months and crash data for up to 90 days.

Advertising

Google AdMob supplies ads: optional rewarded videos that grant an in-game tool, and occasional full-screen ads between solo games. Ads can be removed with a one-time purchase. The AdMob SDK may process device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP-derived general location, ad interactions and diagnostic data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and applicable US states, Google's consent message (UMP) asks for your choices; use Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices to change them. Your Android advertising-ID settings also apply.

Purchases

Google Play handles payments, order records, refunds and purchase restoration. JoyDeck receives product and ownership information, never your payment-card details.

Third parties

Google processes data under its own terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More about how Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Permissions

Internet and network-state access support ads, consent, analytics and purchases. The advertising SDK may use the advertising-ID permission. JoyDeck does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access. All games can be played offline.

Children

JoyDeck is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and your choices

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. You can turn analytics off, change ad privacy choices, reset local progress, or ask us to delete data — see Data deletion.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date above shows the current version.
